# OW-2026-029 — Relay ignored a reply that arrived before the write finished

**Severity: low.** An adapter can receive a matching relay reply while its outgoing write is still returning. The prior client attached a listener only after the write completed. Its event stream discarded that reply. A publish then reported a timeout after 60 seconds. Subscribing to one topic or a batch did the same after 30 seconds. A dApp request could therefore appear to fail after the relay had already acknowledged it. The test establishes an SDK availability problem. It does not measure the frequency on a deployed relay or demonstrate a duplicate blockchain transaction.

## Reproduction

At the [regression baseline](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/blob/439011c1774a5b5e22e75388ab59b67b3443b7a6/Vendor/Reown/Sources/WalletConnectRelay/RelayClient.swift#L145), compare the send and listener at lines 145–149, 203–209, 223–229 and 239–247. The fixture sends the real RPC response through the dispatcher's incoming-message callback synchronously inside its write. There is only one matching success response. An unrelated ID and, for publish and single-subscribe, a wrong result kind exercise the existing routing filters. A second set of cases delivers an early response and then throws from the write. The send error must reach the caller and no subscription may be installed.

The baseline times out in all three early-success cases. The three send-error controls pass. After the repair all six pass, including the topic-to-ID lookup for single and batch subscriptions. The fixture uses synthetic topics, a memory-backed history, and no external endpoint.

## Repair

The [fixed relay client](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/blob/25bcf5bd3418c574d92d63c7d5d5acef39d7d75c/Vendor/Reown/Sources/WalletConnectRelay/RelayClient.swift#L226) installs a listener for the first matching result before sending. One in-memory slot retains that reply through the write. The client waits for the write to succeed before acting on a reply, and begins the response timeout only at that point. The listener is cancelled when the operation exits. The change does not alter the wire format or persisted history.

The separate inbound-notification acknowledgement path, rejection/error payload interpretation, incomplete subscription-ID lists, and cancellation of an already running socket write still require review. Acceptance by a relay is distinct from peer receipt, signing and on-chain execution.
