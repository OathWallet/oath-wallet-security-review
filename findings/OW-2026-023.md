## Repair and evidence

Fix: `a04bebfafd38f02a441c1a0ee81540882438a99c`.

[PR 33](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/pull/33) merged as `fab9c23e02060aa5a9e98f5000808c85fd14f28e`.
Application source matches the tested pin; 97 concurrent website-only
differences are recorded in [merge evidence](../evidence/auth-boundary-merge-identity.json).

Initialization, decoding and outgoing expiry construction share the existing
300–604800-second TTL bounds. The direct expiry constructor throws. Legacy EVM
senders validate every chain and nonemptiness before key creation, and the outer
switcher validates before pairing creation. The general model retains other
chain families for newer flows; valid payloads and routing remain unchanged.

Same 20 production cases: 32 assertions across eight failed cases, eight crashes
and four passing controls before; all 20 pass after. The full SDK suite passes
185 cases with one skip; diagnostics pass 118 with two skips. The fixture and
baseline disclose interface-only dependency injection for memory-only KMS and
transport spies. No copied sender logic or real provider operation is used.

See [review scope and reproduction](../review/AUTH-REQUEST-BOUNDARIES.md) and
[original logs/results](../evidence/auth-boundary/manifest.json). Oath's inspected
app source does not call the deprecated outgoing authentication API; no remotely
triggered app exploit is claimed. Link-response routing, transport-failure cleanup
and broader authentication semantics remain explicitly incomplete. This is a
source repair, not a verified App Store update.
