# OW-2026-040 — East Asian word boundaries in Electrum two-factor recovery

Moderate severity, conditional recovery and wallet identity. Electrum's historical TrustedCoin format splits an input of at least 20 words into two cosigner phrases before separately normalizing each half. Its post-2.7 12-word format instead derives from the whole seed. Adjacent CJK characters lose their intervening spaces during Electrum KDF normalization.

At the progress 53 target Oath counted words to recognize the version but split the already-normalized string for derivation. A valid synthetic 24-word input returned the wrong primary material and an absent second cosigner. Two independent separator variants caused four assertion failures. The app's import and persistence paths also passed normalized text back to its decoder. The published test-only commit has identical shipped source to the target. Tests used deterministic fixtures and no funded wallets. This establishes a compatibility and recovery problem for matching inputs. It does not establish a count of affected wallets.
