# OW-2026-042 — A sliced one-byte note could terminate transaction planning

Low severity, local availability. Swift Foundation Data slices retain their original start index. The public Bitcoin send planner accepted a one-byte slice, then attempted to read its zeroth index to choose a minimal push opcode. A local XCTest process trapped. The debugger identified the Foundation subscript, the BitcoinSendOptions.dataScript getter and BitcoinSendPlan.make in the stack. The current app text entry constructs fresh Data; reachability through that text field was not demonstrated.

The encoder now reads the collection’s first byte. A test of the normal planning and validation entry points checks four single-byte values from nonzero-based slices against their ordinary encodings. The repaired case and the pre-existing payload size/control case both completed.
