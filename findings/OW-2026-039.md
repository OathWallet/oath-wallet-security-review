# OW-2026-039 — A valid BIP39 recovery could be hidden by aezeed recognition

> Current audit target: [5.0.2 (104) prerelease](../review/RELEASE-5.0.2-104.md). Earlier results below retain their original tested versions and commits; they are not retroactively tests of build 104.

Low severity, recovery availability and identity. One valid LND phrase also passes the BIP39 checksum and derives a different Bitcoin receive address as BIP39. The original picker suppressed the alternative, and the wallet engine rejected the BIP39 route even after dictionary validation. Explicit format selection now survives validation and is bound to the current text.
