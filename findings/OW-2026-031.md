# OW-2026-031 — Numeric hosts bypassed artwork URL restrictions

> Current audit target: [5.0.2 (104) prerelease](../review/RELEASE-5.0.2-104.md). Earlier results below retain their original tested versions and commits; they are not retroactively tests of build 104.

**Severity: low, URL admission and exposure to unintended destinations.** A provider-supplied token logo could name an IPv4 literal or residential DNS name that the wallet intended to exclude. A redirect or thumbnail could introduce the same destination. A request reaching a private service depends on the resolver, routing, TLS and the device. No private service, successful TLS session, secret disclosure or fund movement is part of the evidence.

The [original host gate](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/blob/7da0cd82a81bce01a0f6ed29ccae9d33f5c13a95/Packages/OathTokenImages/Sources/OathArtworkSource/ArtworkSource.swift#L33) rejected dotted decimal strings but allowed hex/mixed-base hosts. Darwin's numeric parser interprets `0x7f.1` as loopback and `0xA9.0xfe.0.1` as a link-local address. The policy also admitted malformed DNS labels and home.arpa names. Its output fed both the loader and the redirect callback.

The [repair](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/blob/d0c6b3a73ec794d7b768bb71454ba17c0063af98/Packages/OathTokenImages/Sources/OathArtworkSource/ArtworkSource.swift#L34) validates the full decoded hostname, including label boundaries and DNS lengths. International labels receive Foundation's IDNA representation. The guard filters special-use suffixes and rejects numeric literals recognized by Darwin. C-string conversion happens only after rejecting non-DNS characters, including NUL. Compatibility cases exercise ordinary and internationalized CDN names, escaped Unicode, public redirect targets, HTTP-to-HTTPS normalization, and the established IPFS and Arweave gateways.

The identical [eight-case fixture](https://github.com/devdasx/oath-wallet/blob/7da0cd82a81bce01a0f6ed29ccae9d33f5c13a95/Packages/OathTokenImages/Tests/OathTokenImagesTests/ArtworkHostPolicyTests.swift) runs against both commits in separate network-denied processes. Before the repair six cases failed (16 assertions), and both controls passed. The original loader forwarded the synthetic loopback spelling to an in-memory transport. Its redirect delegate returned the unwanted target. After repair all eight cases pass. The URLSession task used to exercise the delegate is never resumed. The proof uses no DNS lookup or socket connection.

Run `python3 scripts/artwork-host-policy.py <clean-app> --scratch .work/artwork-host-build --output .work/artwork-host`. [Network and upstream limits](../review/ARTWORK-HOSTS-AND-DOM.md).
