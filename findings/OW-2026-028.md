## Fix and limits

Fix `4cb121a588583b9b0adef626c05cbedafce3a759` adds a local optional persisted generation to history records and
conditional resolution/deletion under the history lock. Existing records remain
readable and RPC wire payloads do not gain this field. See [compatibility and
commands](../review/HISTORY-SEND-GENERATIONS.md) and [hashed original evidence](../evidence/history-generation/manifest.json).

SDK 254 passed/one skip, diagnostics 118 passed/two skips, unsigned iOS Release
compile passed. Synthetic endpoints exercise complete production transport and
history paths; repeated relay ACKs intentionally isolate a separate ordering
question. No live relay or full peer handshake is tested. Already-delivered
messages cannot be recalled; pre-send authorization, acknowledgement bookkeeping,
older concurrent writers and other open areas remain documented.
